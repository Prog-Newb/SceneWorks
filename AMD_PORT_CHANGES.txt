AMD / ROCm port of SceneWorks 0.8.7.

Adds Radeon support as a third backend beside MLX and Candle/CUDA, selected at
build time by the `backend-rocm` feature. Validated on an RX 7900 XTX (gfx1100)
under Windows: SeedVR2 image and video upscaling both run on ROCm at
15.3 s/frame for 1440p video.

The CUDA and MLX lanes are unchanged. Against the 0.8.7 baseline this touches
35 upstream files for +1018 / -161 lines, and no file is net-subtractive.

Documentation:
- docs/amd-port/README.md                 scope and prerequisites
- docs/amd-port/PORT_MAP.md               architecture and the processing chain
- docs/amd-port/README-AMD-TESTER.md      how to run it, and the results
- docs/amd-port/IMPLEMENTATION-NOTES.md   the detailed account

Three of the changes are NOT AMD-specific and apply to every platform:
- a rectangular spatial tiler, a conv3d `T==1` collapse and a fused GroupNorm,
  together 4.0x on this card, all backend-agnostic (candle-gen-seedvr2)
- multi-window video progress, which reported 41% at 1.1% done
- the video ETA, which existed end to end but was always null, plus a disk
  guard that over-estimated output size by 4.1x

One deliberate restriction, scoped to the new lane only: a ROCm worker serves
SeedVR2 and refuses Real-ESRGAN and dataset_upscale, because those run through
`ort` and there is no HIP execution provider. The refusal is in the router
(`worker_supports_job`, gated on `worker_is_rocm`); the Mac and candle lanes
keep every engine and capability they had.

The ROCm backend inside the vendored candle fork is third-party work (17 of 25
commits on that fork); candle is MIT / Apache-2.0.
